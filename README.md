# TS Academy Data Science-Hajime Cohort Group 25
Bank Marketing - From Raw Data to Real Predictions. 

Project [Dataset].

## Task
> You have joined the data team at a company with a dataset and a hunch. They believe patterns are
hiding in it that could change how they treat customers, price products, or allocate resources, but nobody is
sure what those patterns are. They want two things: an honest map of the natural groups in the data, and a
model that predicts the outcome they care about.
Work as a team, run your assigned dataset through the full pipeline you learned this term, and submit one
notebook that tells the whole story. Every group must complete every stage. You cannot skip clustering,
classification, or imbalance handling; each is graded on its own.

[Dataset]: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/janiobachmann/bank-marketing-dataset
